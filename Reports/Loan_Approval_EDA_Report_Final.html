13. Preprocessing Steps

  • Trimmed spaces from column names and categorical text values.
  • Checked and removed duplicate rows.
  • Filtered loan_amount outliers using the IQR rule.
  • Encoded education: Graduate = 1, Not Graduate = 0.
  • Encoded self_employed: Yes = 1, No = 0.
  • Encoded loan_status: Approved = 1, Rejected = 0.
  • Removed loan_id from model features.
  • Used an 80/20 stratified split and StandardScaler.

14. Conclusion

For this dataset and the specified train/test split, Logistic Regression achieved accuracy of 91.33%, precision of 92.08%, recall of 94.16%, and F1 score of 93.11%.

These are results from an academic dataset experiment and do not guarantee real-world loan decisions.
